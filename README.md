# Privacy Policy & Data Protection

This Case Study  is a simple study of **privacy policies and data protection** in organizations.

It explains how companies collect, use, store, and protect people's personal information. The project covers important topics such as **data protection principles, data subject rights, cross-border data transfers, data breaches, and privacy governance**.

It also explains how organizations can improve their privacy policies, protect personal data, follow applicable laws, and build trust with customers.

The main aim of this project is to understand why **privacy and data protection are important in today's digital world**.
